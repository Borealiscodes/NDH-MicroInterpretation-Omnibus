## ⭐ 2 — The Reflection Layer (Master)  
In the original v1.0 build, the reflection layer served as:

- a **Zen‑geometry synthesis engine**  
- a **narrative compression field**  
- a **prismatic interpretive surface**  
- a **mirror for novice–master exchanges**  

It was designed to “complete” the Anthology and Codex layers by providing a **reflective narrative**.

This was elegant — but structurally dangerous.

## ⭐ 3 — Pre‑Diagnostic Reflection Assumptions  
Before diagnostics, the reflection layer assumed:

- reflection is inherently **non‑recursive**  
- narrative compression cannot **elevate altitude**  
- metaphorical curvature is **safe**  
- reflective commentary cannot **self‑describe**  
- koan‑style loops are **symbolic**, not structural  

All of these assumptions were later disproven.

- **narrative‑geometry fusion**  

These hazards were not immediately visible in v1.0, but became catastrophic in v1.1.
